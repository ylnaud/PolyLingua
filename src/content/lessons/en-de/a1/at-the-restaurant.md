Eating out is one of the most common situations where you will need German on the spot.
This lesson covers the phrases that carry you from sitting down to paying the bill.

## Getting started: the menu

- Die Speisekarte, bitte. — The menu, please.
- Was empfehlen Sie? — What do you recommend?
- Haben Sie einen Tisch für zwei? — Do you have a table for two?

## Ordering politely: "Ich hätte gern..."

The most natural way to order in German is **Ich hätte gern...** (literally "I would
have gladly...", meaning "I would like..."). It works the same way as "Ich möchte", but
is especially common in restaurants:

```
Ich hätte gern einen Kaffee.   I would like a coffee.
Ich hätte gern die Suppe.       I would like the soup.
Ich hätte gern das Schnitzel.   I would like the schnitzel.
```

Notice der Kaffee (masculine) becomes **einen** Kaffee — masculine nouns change their
article to "einen" when they are the direct object of the sentence. Feminine and neuter
nouns (die Suppe, das Schnitzel) do not change this way at the beginner level.

## Before you eat

- **Guten Appetit!** — Enjoy your meal! (said by anyone at the table before eating)
- **Zum Wohl! / Prost!** — Cheers! (when raising a drink)

## Asking for the bill

Two common, equally natural ways:

- Die Rechnung, bitte. — The bill, please.
- Zahlen, bitte. — To pay, please. (a shorter, very common alternative)

If you are in a group, the waiter will likely ask:

- **Getrennt oder zusammen?** — Separate or together? (asking how you want to split the bill)

## Core restaurant vocabulary

| German | English |
| -------- | ------- |
| die Speisekarte | the menu |
| bestellen | to order |
| der Kellner / die Kellnerin | the waiter / waitress |
| die Rechnung | the bill |
| das Trinkgeld | the tip |
| lecker | tasty / delicious |

## Sample conversation

```
A: Guten Tag! Was hätten Sie gern?     Hello! What would you like?
B: Ich hätte gern die Suppe, bitte.     I would like the soup, please.
A: Und zu trinken?                       And to drink?
B: Ein Wasser, bitte.                    A water, please.
```

Practice ordering your favorite foods out loud using "Ich hätte gern..." — it is one of
the most immediately usable phrases you will learn in this course.
