Talking about hobbies is a natural, friendly conversation topic in German — and it
introduces one of the most useful small words in the language: **gern**.

## The gern pattern: how German says "I like"

Unlike English, German has no single verb that works exactly like "to like" for
activities. Instead, it uses the conjugated main verb plus the word **gern** (gladly),
placed directly after the verb:

```
Ich spiele gern Fußball.    I like playing soccer.   (literally: I play gladly soccer)
Ich lese gern.                I like to read.           (literally: I read gladly)
Ich höre gern Musik.          I like listening to music. (literally: I listen gladly to music)
```

Once you get used to this word order — verb, then gern, then the rest of the sentence —
you can express liking almost any activity you already know the verb for.

## Asking about hobbies

- Was machst du gern? — What do you like to do?
- Was ist dein Hobby? — What is your hobby?
- Machst du gern Sport? — Do you like doing sports?

## Common hobby verbs and phrases

| German | English |
| -------- | ------- |
| lesen | to read |
| schwimmen | to swim |
| kochen | to cook |
| malen | to paint |
| tanzen | to dance |
| Musik hören | to listen to music |
| Sport treiben | to do/play sports (in general) |
| Fußball spielen | to play soccer |

Notice **Sport treiben** is a fixed expression for doing sports generally, while naming
a specific sport uses "spielen" (to play) — Fußball spielen, Tennis spielen — or its own
specific verb, like schwimmen.

## Free time vocabulary

| German | English |
| -------- | ------- |
| die Freizeit | free time |
| das Hobby | hobby |
| das Wochenende | the weekend |
| gern | gladly (marks liking an activity) |
| oft / manchmal / nie | often / sometimes / never |

## Sample conversation

```
A: Was machst du gern in deiner Freizeit?   What do you like to do in your free time?
B: Ich lese gern und ich schwimme gern.       I like reading and I like swimming. Und du?
A: Ich spiele gern Fußball am Wochenende.     I like playing soccer on the weekend.
```

Try answering "Was machst du gern?" for yourself out loud, using at least two hobbies —
it is a question you will be asked constantly as you meet new German speakers.
