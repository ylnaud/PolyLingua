Talking on the phone in another language is hard because you can't see the other
person's face. The upside is that **almost everything is fixed script**: the same
phrases, always in the same order.

## Answering and introducing yourself

```
Guten Tag, Müller.        Hello, Müller.       (when answering)
Hallo, hier ist Anna.     Hello, this is Anna. (when calling)
```

Two things that surprise learners:

1. Germans answer by saying **their last name**, not "hello." A flat `Müller.` on the
   other end is completely normal.
2. To introduce yourself, you say **`hier ist`** + your name, literally "here is
   Anna." Never `ich bin Anna` on the phone.

## Asking for someone

```
Kann ich bitte mit Herrn Müller sprechen?    Can I speak with Mr. Müller, please?
— Einen Moment, bitte.                        One moment, please.
— Er ist gerade nicht da.                     He's not here right now.
```

Notice `mit Herrn Müller`: after `mit`, some words change form — `Herr` becomes
`Herrn`.

## If they're not there

```
Kann ich eine Nachricht hinterlassen?    Can I leave a message?
Können Sie ihm etwas ausrichten?         Can you pass along a message to him?
Ich rufe später zurück.                  I'll call back later.
```

`zurückrufen` is **separable**: `zurück` goes to the end — `Ich rufe später
**zurück**`.

## When something goes wrong

```
Ich habe mich verwählt.          I dialed the wrong number.
Die Verbindung ist schlecht.     The connection is bad.
Ich kann Sie nicht gut hören.    I can't hear you well.
```

## Saying goodbye

```
Vielen Dank, auf Wiederhören.
```

**Not** `auf Wiedersehen`. `sehen` means "to see" and `hören` means "to hear": since
you don't see each other on the phone, the correct goodbye is `auf Wiederhören`.

## How to practice

Rehearse the whole call out loud: you answer, introduce yourself, ask for someone,
they say the person's not there, you leave a message, and say goodbye. Six sentences
in a row, always the same ones. Once the whole script comes out naturally, the phone
stops being scary.
