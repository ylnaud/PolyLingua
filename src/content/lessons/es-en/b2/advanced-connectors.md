Estos conectores son la señal más clara de un inglés escrito y hablado con estructura
argumentativa clara — esenciales para ensayos, presentaciones y debates.

## Los conectores por función

| Función                           | Conectores              | Ejemplo                                        |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Contraste                         | however, nevertheless   | The plan failed. However, we learned a lot.    |
| Concesión (+ sustantivo/gerundio) | despite, in spite of    | Despite the cost, we bought it.                |
| Concesión (+ frase completa)      | although, even though   | Although it's expensive, we bought it.         |
| Adición                           | moreover, furthermore   | The product is cheap. Moreover, it's reliable. |
| Consecuencia                      | therefore, consequently | She studied hard; therefore, she passed.       |

## La trampa: despite/in spite of vs. although

```
✅ Despite the rain, we went out.               (sustantivo)
✅ Despite raining, we went out.                 (gerundio)
❌ Despite it was raining, we went out.          (incorrecto: no puede ir con frase completa)

✅ Although it was raining, we went out.         (frase completa)
```

## Posición y puntuación

Estos conectores suelen ir al inicio de una nueva frase (con punto y coma o punto
antes), seguidos de coma:

```
The team worked hard. However, they didn't win.
The team worked hard; however, they didn't win.
```

## Combinándolos en un párrafo argumentativo

```
The proposal has clear benefits. Moreover, it would save money in the long run.
However, implementing it would require significant initial investment. Despite this
challenge, the board decided to move forward.
```

## Por qué son la clave del B2

Sin estos conectores, tus ideas suenan como frases sueltas. Con ellos, tu inglés suena
estructurado y persuasivo — la diferencia entre listar ideas y construir un argumento.
